# Changelog

## 1.2

- **Area select press key**: a new setting. Press the key once, then drag, the same as clicking
  the Select area button.
- **Auto-copy names**: a checkbox under the notepad (and a setting) that copies the names to your
  clipboard whenever players are added.
- The hold key setting now suggests which keys work best, and the Select area button mentions you
  can set a key.
- The "Esc to cancel" hint now only shows when Esc actually cancels.

## 1.1

- **Saved notepads.** Keep as many notepads as you like, each with its own title. They save as
  you type, so switching between them never loses anything. Your existing notepad becomes the
  first one, called "Notepad".
- **All notepads** lists them newest first, with how many names each holds and when it was last
  edited. Search by title or by any name inside them.
- **New notepad**, **Rename**, **Duplicate** (a copy of the open notepad, to reuse a team list)
  and **Delete**, which asks first.
- **Clan members only**: a checkbox under the notepad (and a setting) so Select area only adds
  players in your clan. Right-clicking a player still always adds them.
- **Copy names** copies just the names, one per line, leaving out your notes.
- The open notepad shows how many names it has.

## 1.0.1

- Fixed players being dropped from a drag selection. They'd lose their yellow outline once the box fully covered them, and weren't added to the notepad.
